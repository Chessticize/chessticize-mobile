Accessibility

What the current app supports

Chessticize includes labeled controls and adaptable iPhone and iPad layouts, but we do not claim complete support for an accessibility feature until every common chess puzzle task passes.

Screen readers and voice

Menus, tabs, buttons, and many status updates have useful labels. The puzzle board itself does not yet provide a complete VoiceOver or Voice Control move-entry path, so those features are not declared in the App Store.

Text and appearance

Very large system text can clip or overlap controls, and the app does not yet provide a complete dark interface. Larger Text and Dark Interface are therefore not declared.

Visual and motion checks

Contrast, grayscale operation, touch targets, and Reduce Motion behavior have open follow-up work. We will publish a feature declaration only after the unchanged live app or a new release passes every required puzzle flow.

Orientation and iPad

iPhone uses portrait. iPad supports portrait and landscape, with landscape as the primary large-screen layout. Large-text fixes are still required in both size classes.

Share accessibility feedback

Tell us which puzzle flow, device, and assistive setting you used. Public reports help others follow progress; private questions can go to support.
