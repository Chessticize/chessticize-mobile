## Customer-facing changes

- App Store review prompts now appear after fewer positive completed practice runs (two across two dates, or three total), including unrated Sprints, high-accuracy Focused Runs, and strong Survival runs, with a 30-day cooldown.
- Pausing a Sprint by backgrounding the app mid-session no longer suppresses a review prompt when you return and win that Sprint.
