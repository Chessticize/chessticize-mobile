Chessticize 1.3.5 saves the puzzle active when the Sprint clock reaches zero as
Incomplete, keeps it out of the mistake count, and makes it easy to find and
mark as Unclear in History. Arrow Duel Replay guidance also appears immediately
without shifting the board.
