  Duel.

  themes faster.
- Keep all 1.4 million offline puzzles with a smaller bundled puzzle library.

Chessticize 1.4 deepens Arrow Duel with an opponent-reply challenge, makes
saved Runs easier to reorder and configure, and keeps the complete 1.4 million
puzzle offline library in a smaller bundled database.
